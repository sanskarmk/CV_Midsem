Comprehensive exam — pattern & what to drill

Stated live by Prof. Adhikary (with Prof. Sugata) in the object-tracking / wrap-up session. This is the open-book paper: manuals and PDFs are allowed, so they will not ask you to vomit a derivation. They will ask whether you know which tool to grab and why.

✦ Pattern — quoted from the recording
  • EC-3 = 40% of the course (40-mark paper). EC-1 30%, EC-2 (midsem) 30%.
  • 5–7 questions (they joked they are “not heartless” — not 8). Uneven marks: some 4, some 6–7, some 9–10. A correctly solved 10-marker is a “low-hanging fruit.”
  • 10–20% of the paper is pre-midsem (Sessions 1–8 / up to RANSAC). 80–90% is post-midsem (classification → ViT → seg → detect → track → metrics → BoW / edge / optional).
  • Question types: system design, latency, numericals that mix topics. “We are not going to test you for rote learning, because you already have your manuals. If you know, you will answer; if you don’t, you won’t.”
  • Numericals “similar in kind” to the watermarked slides / sample paper — not copy-paste of the same numbers.
  • Open book: publisher textbooks + the material you carry. Concept first: “why this approach, why not the other.”
▲ He also said, on slides vs handout

A student asked: the handout numbering and the taught topics don’t match. Answer, in spirit: study from more than one resource (slides + recordings + this site + T1/T2). The comprehensive covers all topics in the handout, including webinar/optional modules. If it was taught or listed, it’s fair game — but 80–90% of marks sit after RANSAC.

How to sit the open book

Tab this site
Session pages for the “why,” formulas for the one-liners, numericals for the method.
Write assumptions
Same midsem habit: axes, IoU threshold, \(k\), binning, which YOLO version (v1 grid vs later anchors — he said v2 anchors are out of syllabus).
Upgrade sentences
Keep a one-liner for each “why better than predecessor”: Fast vs R-CNN, Faster vs Fast, YOLO vs Faster, DoG vs LoG, ViT vs ResNet (data diet), MobileNet vs plain conv.
Don’t derive
Attention / Kalman full algebra: “conceptually present; full brunt not in syllabus.” Sketch QKV and predict–update; move on.
