DemoNumericals SESSION 5 Canny & Hough Transform The 4-stage Canny pipeline and the Hough voting scheme for lines — both (m, c) and (ρ, θ) parameterisations. DemoNumericals SESSION 6 Harris Corners & HOG Structure tensor, eigenvalue intuition and the corner response R; plus the Histogram of Oriented Gradients descriptor. DemoNumericals SESSION 7 SIFT & RANSAC Scale-space, Difference-of-Gaussians, keypoints & descriptors that survive scale/rotation — and robust fitting with RANSAC. Concepts SESSION 9 Image Classification Train/val/test, bag of words, kNN, linear classifiers, softmax vs binary vs multilabel cross-entropy. DemoNumericals SESSION 10 Attention, Transformers & ViT Q/K/V, scaled dot-product, multi-head, positional embeddings, and the Vision Transformer pipeline. What the exams look like

✦ Two papers, two patterns

Midsem (EC-2, 30%, closed book): 4–5 unequal questions, theory+numerical, syllabus = Sessions 1–8 up to RANSAC. Drill Exam Prep and From the Lectures.

Comprehensive (EC-3, 40%, open book): 5–7 uneven questions (4–10 marks), 80–90% after RANSAC, mix of system-design, latency and mixed numericals — not rote. Full briefing on the Comprehensive page.

How to use this site

① Read for intuition

Start each topic with the Intuition callouts. If you can explain the idea in one sentence, the formula will stick.

② Play the demos

Drag a kernel across an image, watch Hough votes pile up, step through Canny. Seeing it move beats memorizing.

③ Re-derive numericals

Cover the solution, try it, then check. Speed on these is what the midsem rewards.
